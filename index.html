01 / About

A career, compressed. 二十年,一段行业的缩影

Position
定位

从传统广播电视、卫视综艺、超级网综,到流媒体与 Creator Economy—— 我经历了过去二十年中国内容产业完整的工业化演进。从一线剪辑师起步,到 70+ 人全建制后期团队的运营者,再到现在专注于 AI 与后期工业化的下一代 Pipeline。

From magnetic tape to streaming, from Final Cut Pro 7 to AI-augmented editorial — I've operated at every layer of post-production scale, with a particular focus on the systems that let creative teams move fast without breaking.

在我看来,后期总监的本质不是剪辑师的延伸,而是一个内容工业体系的架构师—— 协调团队、流程、技术、交付,让创作的能量稳定地穿过工业化管道,变成观众看到的成品。

"Because I taught the Mac post-production workflow at Enlight Media, colleagues began calling me 'Mac' — the name that followed me through eighteen years of post-production.
When I started building tools for editors, I became Ethan Mai — and the work goes by Cutsmith.
Same person. Two chapters."
